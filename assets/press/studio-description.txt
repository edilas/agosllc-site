AGOS LLC is an independent, family-run studio based in California, creating original games and practical apps. Its products include Fingularity, the celestial swipe game; Starfall: Shattered Continuum, a space shooter; and TACH, a focused device information utility. Team Sinigang brings together the studio’s games and Filipino-rooted culture, while Agos Forge is its home for software experiments and projects taking shape, including EverydayKeep. “Ideas in motion. From flow to form.”

Android status as of October 18, 2026: TACH and Starfall are in Google Play review and are not publicly available on Google Play.
