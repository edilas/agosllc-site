Starfall: Shattered Continuum is a touch-controlled space shooter. Dodge enemy fire, earn your fleet, and fight through a 200-level campaign of escalating cosmic threats.

Push deeper into the Continuum, with a boss encounter every five levels and challenges beyond the Dreadnought milestone.

Where is Starfall available?

Starfall is now available on Google Play for Android. The iPhone release has been submitted to Apple and is awaiting App Store review.

What will the free game include?

The Android release is free to start with levels 1–10, including the first two boss encounters. One optional purchase unlocks levels 11–200. Ships, upgrades, and milestone rewards are earned through play.
